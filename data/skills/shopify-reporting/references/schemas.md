# ShopifyQL schemas

Every schema and the metrics it owns, from the 2026-07 reference. A metric one
schema owns does not exist in another. Dimensions are listed only for `sessions`;
for other schemas read `https://shopify.dev/docs/api/shopifyql/latest/schemas` and
append `/<category>/<schema>.md`.

## `sessions` dimensions

- Time — `hour` `day` `week` `month` `quarter` `year` `hour_of_day` `day_of_week` `week_of_year` `month_of_year`
- Traffic — `referrer_source` `referrer_domain` `referrer_name` `referrer_path` `referrer_url` `referrer_terms` `referring_channel` `referring_medium` `referring_platform` `traffic_type` `agentic_referring_channel`
- Campaign — `utm_source` `utm_medium` `utm_campaign` `utm_content` `utm_term`
- Landing — `landing_page_path` `landing_page_type` `landing_page_url`
- Device — `session_device_type` `session_device_browser` `session_device_browser_version` `session_device_os` `session_device_os_version`
- Geo — `session_country` `session_country_code` `session_region` `session_city`
- Quality — `human_or_bot_session` `session_bounced` `session_api_client` `session_duration` `session_id`
- Customer — `customer_id` `customer_email` `customer_name` `customer_number_of_orders` `customer_amount_spent` `customer_tags` `rfm_group` `predicted_spend_tier` `customer_cohort_month` `first_order_sales_channel`

`agentic_referring_channel` attributes sessions to AI assistants: ChatGPT, Google AI
Mode and Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Shop.

`MATCHES` filters on related records — `customer.orders_placed`,
`customer.products_purchased`, `customer.storefront_event.product_viewed`,
`customer.shopify_email.*`, `customer.store_credit_accounts`,
`customer.within_distance`.
