title: HyperFrames
description: Create, animate, and render videos from HTML compositions
longDescription: Turn a brief, a URL, a PR, or a Figma design into a rendered
  video. HyperFrames composes video as HTML — the DOM declares timing, the
  animation runtime is seekable, and the framework owns media playback — so Ren
  can author, preview, and batch-render motion graphics, explainers, captioned
  clips, title cards, slideshows, and product launch videos without leaving the
  project. Resolve music, voiceover, captions, images, and brand assets through
  one media layer, port existing Remotion projects, and publish finished renders
  locally or through HeyGen's cloud.
